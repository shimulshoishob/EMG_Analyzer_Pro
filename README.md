## 🌟 Executive Overview

**EMG-Analyzer-Pro** is an automated validation and diagnostic engine for multi-channel surface electromyography (sEMG) acquisition systems (defaulting to 8-channel, 500 Hz setups). 

Surface EMG captures microvolt-level electrical potentials generated by muscle fiber depolarization during motor unit recruitment. Because sEMG signals are highly vulnerable to electromagnetic interference, motion artifacts, electrode detachment, baseline drift, and cross-talk, automated quality assessment is vital before deploying data into biomechanical analysis or machine learning pipelines (e.g., prosthetic control, gesture recognition, rehabilitation monitoring).

### 1. Sampling, Timing Integrity & Nyquist Criteria

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\Delta t_{\mathrm{exp}} = \frac{1000}{F_s} \quad [\mathrm{ms}], \qquad f_{\mathrm{Nyq}} = \frac{F_s}{2} \quad [\mathrm{Hz}]$$

$$\Delta t_i = t_{i+1} - t_i \quad [\mathrm{ms}]$$

$$\mathrm{Duration} = \frac{t_N - t_1}{1000} \quad [\mathrm{s}], \qquad \mathrm{Coverage} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{N_{\mathrm{actual}}}{N_{\mathrm{expected}}}$$

- $F_s$: Nominal sampling frequency in Hertz ($\mathrm{Hz}$).
- $\Delta t_{\mathrm{exp}}$: Theoretical inter-sample time step in milliseconds ($\mathrm{ms}$).
- $f_{\mathrm{Nyq}}$: Nyquist critical frequency ($\mathrm{Hz}$).
- $t_i$: Timestamp of sample $i$ in milliseconds.
- $N_{\mathrm{actual}}, N_{\mathrm{expected}}$: Number of acquired samples versus expected samples from metadata.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Surface EMG signals exhibit dominant power between $10\text{ Hz}$ and $400\text{ Hz}$, with the primary energy envelope residing within $20\text{ Hz} - 200\text{ Hz}$. According to the **Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem**, the sampling rate $F_s$ must exceed at least twice the maximum frequency component ($F_s \ge 2 f_{\max}$) to prevent irreversible spectral aliasing. At $F_s = 500\text{ Hz}$, $f_{\mathrm{Nyq}} = 250\text{ Hz}$, which adequately preserves primary motor unit action potential (MUAP) dynamics.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
- **Non-uniform Sampling & Jitter ($\Delta t_i \neq \Delta t_{\mathrm{exp}}$)**: Variable inter-sample intervals warp the frequency domain during Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and Welch Power Spectral Density calculations.
- **Backward Steps ($\Delta t_i < 0$) & Duplicates ($\Delta t_i = 0$)**: Indicate asynchronous microcontroller buffer overruns or timestamp rollover errors.
- **Low Coverage ($<95\%$)**: Truncated sessions yield insufficient gesture repetitions, skewing trial-level statistical validity.

### 2. Packet Continuity & Data Loss Analysis

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\Delta p_i = p_{i+1} - p_i$$

$$N_{\mathrm{missing}} = \sum_{\Delta p_i > 1} (\Delta p_i - 1)$$

$$\mathrm{Missing\,Rate} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{N_{\mathrm{missing}}}{N_{\mathrm{unique}} + N_{\mathrm{missing}}}$$

- $p_i$: Sequential integer packet ID transmitted by the microcontroller.
- $N_{\mathrm{unique}}$: Total count of unique packets received.
- $N_{\mathrm{missing}}$: Total count of lost packets deduced from packet ID jumps.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Hardware sensors transmit digitized ADC frames via serial interfaces (UART, Bluetooth, SPI/I2C, USB). When wireless interference occurs or baud-rate buffers saturate, entire data frames are dropped.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
Packet drops create abrupt step discontinuities in the time-domain signal. When passed through digital recursive IIR/Butterworth filters, these discontinuities introduce large **filter ringing artifacts** (Gibbs phenomenon) and distort feature extraction windows. A packet missing rate $> 1.0\%$ raises a system warning.

### 3. Channel Amplitude & Hardware Baseline Diagnostics

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\mu = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} x_i, \qquad \sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(x_i - \mu)^2}$$

$$\mathrm{RMS}_{\mathrm{raw}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} x_i^2}, \qquad \mathrm{MAV} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} |x_i - \mu|$$

$$\mathrm{P2P} = \max_i(x_i) - \min_i(x_i)$$

$$\mathrm{Zero} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{\#\{x_i \le \mathrm{ADC}_{\min}\}}{N}, \qquad \mathrm{Sat} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{\#\{x_i \ge \mathrm{ADC}_{\max}\}}{N}$$

$$\mathrm{Flatline} \iff \sigma_{\mathrm{1\,s}} < 10^{-6} \quad [\mathrm{counts}]$$

$$r_{xy} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}(x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{N}(x_i - \bar{x})^2 \sum_{i=1}^{N}(y_i - \bar{y})^2}}$$

- $x_i$: Digitized ADC raw count at sample $i$.
- $b$: ADC bit resolution ($b = 10 \implies \mathrm{ADC}_{\max} = 1023$, $\mathrm{ADC}_{\min} = 0$).
- $\mathrm{P2P}$: Dynamic range (peak-to-peak amplitude).
- $r_{xy}$: Pearson cross-correlation coefficient between channels $x$ and $y$.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
- **Mean ($\mu$) & DC Offset**: Unipolar ADCs bias signals to mid-rail ($V_{\mathrm{ref}}/2$). Large baseline shifts away from nominal indicate electrochemical electrode-skin half-cell polarization.
- **Saturation ($\mathrm{Sat}\%$) & Zeros ($\mathrm{Zero}\%$)**: Signal clipping at $V_{\mathrm{DD}}$ or ground ($0\text{V}$) occurs when amplifier gain is set too high for voluntary contraction strength or during motion-induced voltage spikes.
- **Flatlining ($\sigma_{\mathrm{1\,s}} < 10^{-6}$)**: Signifies an open circuit, disconnected lead, or dead channel.
- **Cross-Correlation ($r_{xy}$)**: Measures volume conduction and spatial cross-talk between adjacent muscle bellies. High correlation ($|r_{xy}| > 0.85$) indicates spatial redundancy or short-circuited electrode traces.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
Clipping strips the peaks of action potentials, non-linearly compressing the true amplitude and producing artificial high-frequency harmonic distortion. Channels with high saturation or flatlining receive severe scorecard penalties ($30 - 45\text{ points}$).

### 4. Muscle Activation Dynamics & Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\mathrm{RMS}_c = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(x_i - \mu)^2}$$

$$\mathrm{AR} = \frac{\mathrm{RMS}_c(\mathrm{gesture})}{\mathrm{RMS}_c(\mathrm{rest})}$$

$$\mathrm{SNR}_{\mathrm{dB}} = 20 \log_{10}\left(\frac{\mathrm{RMS}(x_{\mathrm{bp}}^{\mathrm{active}})}{\mathrm{RMS}(x_{\mathrm{bp}}^{\mathrm{rest}})}\right)$$

- $\mathrm{RMS}_c$: Centered Root Mean Square (standard deviation of the signal), eliminating the DC baseline offset.
- $\mathrm{AR}$: Muscle Activation Ratio comparing active muscle contraction to baseline rest.
- $x_{\mathrm{bp}}^{\mathrm{active}}, x_{\mathrm{bp}}^{\mathrm{rest}}$: Zero-phase band-pass filtered EMG signal during gesture and rest intervals.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
When a muscle transitions from resting state to voluntary contraction, somatic motor neurons increase their discharge rate and recruit additional motor units, causing a surge in electrical potential variance ($\mathrm{RMS}_c$). Resting RMS reflects the intrinsic biological and instrumentation noise floor.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
- $\mathrm{AR} \approx 1.0$ ($< 1.5$): Indicates the electrode is placed over an inactive muscle belly, the contraction effort is inadequate, or the electrode has lost contact.
- $\mathrm{SNR}_{\mathrm{dB}} < 6\text{ dB}$: Signals where background noise dominates active contraction cannot be reliably classified by machine learning models.

### 5. Spectral Analysis & Power-Line (Mains) Interference

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$P_{xx}(f) = \frac{1}{K U} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \left| \sum_{n=0}^{L-1} w[n] \, x_k[n] \, e^{-j 2\pi f n / F_s} \right|^2, \qquad U = F_s \sum_{n=0}^{L-1} w[n]^2$$

$$P_{[f_1, f_2]} = \int_{f_1}^{f_2} P_{xx}(f) \, df \approx \sum_{i} \frac{P_i + P_{i+1}}{2} \, \Delta f_i$$

$$\mathrm{Mains\,Interference} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{P_{[f_0-2,\, f_0+2]}}{P_{[1,\, f_{\mathrm{Nyq}}]}}$$

- $P_{xx}(f)$: Welch Power Spectral Density ($\mathrm{V}^2/\mathrm{Hz}$ or $\mathrm{counts}^2/\mathrm{Hz}$).
- $w[n]$: Hann smoothing window of length $L$ ($L=256$ or $128$).
- $K$: Number of overlapping segments ($50\%$ overlap).
- $f_0$: Power-line utility frequency ($50\text{ Hz}$ or $60\text{ Hz}$).
- $P_{[f_1, f_2]}$: Integrated spectral band power evaluated using the trapezoidal rule.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Human skin acts as an antenna picking up capacitive coupling from nearby alternating current (AC) power lines ($50\text{ Hz}$ in EU/Asia, $60\text{ Hz}$ in US). If skin-electrode impedance is unbalanced or reference grounding is poor, large sinusoidal spikes at $f_0$ overwhelm the underlying physiological signal.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
If $\mathrm{Mains} \, \% > 15\%$, power-line hum masks genuine neuromuscular signals in the vital $40-70\text{ Hz}$ motor unit firing range, necessitating skin re-preparation, shielded cabling, or digital notch filtering.

### 6. Digital Filtering, Linear Envelope & Temporal Smoothing

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$|H(j\omega)|^2 = \frac{1}{1 + \left(\frac{\omega}{\omega_c}\right)^{2n}}$$

$$x_{\mathrm{bp}}[n] = \mathrm{filtfilt}\left(H_{\mathrm{BP}}, \, x[n] - \mu\right) \quad \text{where } H_{\mathrm{BP}} = [20\text{ Hz}, 200\text{ Hz}]$$

$$e[n] = \mathrm{filtfilt}\left(H_{\mathrm{LP}}, \, |x_{\mathrm{bp}}[n]|\right) \quad \text{where } H_{\mathrm{LP}} = 5\text{ Hz}$$

- $H(j\omega)$: $n^{\mathrm{th}}$-order Butterworth analog prototype transfer function ($n=4$ for band-pass, $n=2$ for low-pass).
- $\mathrm{filtfilt}$: Forward-backward zero-phase digital filtering using Second-Order Sections (SOS) to avoid phase distortion ($\theta(\omega) = 0$).
- $e[n]$: Smoothed linear amplitude envelope representing muscle contraction force profile.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
- **High-Pass Cutoff ($20\text{ Hz}$)**: Eliminates low-frequency motion artifacts, cable swaying, and baseline wandering.
- **Low-Pass Cutoff ($200\text{ Hz}$)**: Suppresses high-frequency thermal electronic noise and prevents aliasing.
- **Linear Envelope ($5\text{ Hz}$)**: Full-wave rectification ($|x_{\mathrm{bp}}|$) followed by low-pass filtering extracts the low-frequency mechanical contraction profile, mirroring muscle twitch dynamics.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
Zero-phase filtering preserves exact temporal alignment between gesture onsets and biomechanical movement. The envelope provides an intuitive visualization of force generation and muscle activation bursts.

### 7. Spectral Fatigue Tracking (Median & Mean Frequency)

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\sum_{f_i \le f_{\mathrm{med}}} P_i = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^{M} P_i \implies \int_{f_{\mathrm{low}}}^{f_{\mathrm{med}}} P_{xx}(f)\,df = \int_{f_{\mathrm{med}}}^{f_{\mathrm{high}}} P_{xx}(f)\,df$$

$$f_{\mathrm{mean}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{M} f_i \, P_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{M} P_i}$$

- $f_{\mathrm{med}}$ (MDF): Median Frequency ($\mathrm{Hz}$), dividing the power spectrum into two equal halves.
- $f_{\mathrm{mean}}$ (MNF): Mean Frequency ($\mathrm{Hz}$), the power-weighted centroid frequency.
- Evaluated over the active EMG band ($20\text{ Hz} - 200\text{ Hz}$).

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
During sustained or repetitive muscular contraction, metabolic byproducts (e.g., lactic acid, $\mathrm{H}^+$ ions) accumulate within the muscle fibers. This lowers intracellular pH and reduces the **Muscle Fiber Conduction Velocity (MFCV)** of propagating action potentials. As conduction velocity slows, the duration of MUAPs widens in the time domain, which mathematically compresses the power spectrum toward lower frequencies.

```
Frequency Spectrum Compression during Localized Muscle Fatigue:
Power
  ▲
  │     Initial State (Fresh Muscle)       ──► Median Freq ≈ 95 Hz
  │         ┌───┐
  │        /     \
  │       /       \     Fatigued State     ──► Median Freq Shifts Left ≈ 68 Hz
  │      ┌───┐     \        ┌───┐
  │     /  :  \     \      /  :  \
  │    /   :   \     \    /   :   \
  └───┴────┴────┴─────┴──┴────┴────┴────────────────► Frequency (Hz)
         Fatigued MDF   Initial MDF
```

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
A systematic downward drift of $f_{\mathrm{med}}$ across sequential trials serves as a gold-standard physiological indicator of **localized muscle fatigue**. In machine learning pipelines, failing to account for fatigue-induced spectral shift causes classification models to degrade over time.

### 8. Machine Learning Windowing, Fast RMS & Label Purity

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$W = \frac{F_s \cdot T_{\mathrm{win}}}{1000}, \qquad S = \frac{F_s \cdot T_{\mathrm{stride}}}{1000}$$

$$C[n] = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} x_{\mathrm{bp}, i}^2 \quad \text{with } C[0] = 0$$

$$\mathrm{RMS}_s = \sqrt{\frac{C[s + W] - C[s]}{W}}, \qquad \phi_s = \ln(\mathrm{RMS}_s + 10^{-6})$$

$$\mathrm{Purity} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{majority}}}{W} \in [0.0, 1.0]$$

- $T_{\mathrm{win}}, T_{\mathrm{stride}}$: Window length and step size in milliseconds (e.g., $200\text{ ms}$ window, $50\text{ ms}$ stride).
- $W, S$: Window and stride length in discrete samples ($W=100, S=25$ at $500\text{ Hz}$).
- $C[n]$: Prefix-sum cumulative squared array enabling **$\mathcal{O}(1)$ constant-time** window RMS computation.
- $\phi_s$: Log-transformed window RMS feature (stabilizes variance and linearizes exponential activation relationships).
- $\mathrm{Purity}$: Fraction of samples in a window belonging to the dominant gesture label.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
For real-time prosthetic control and interactive gesture recognition, decision latency must remain below $300\text{ ms}$ to avoid perceived lag. Sliding overlapping windows satisfy this controller constraint.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
- **Prefix Sum Algorithm**: Accelerates feature calculation over hundreds of thousands of windows from $\mathcal{O}(N \cdot W)$ to $\mathcal{O}(N)$, enabling instant client-side execution.
- **Label Purity Filtering**: Windows spanning transition boundaries (e.g., Rest $\to$ Fist) contain mixed neuromuscular states. Only windows with $\mathrm{Purity} = 1.0$ ($100\%$) are passed to downstream separability metrics.

### 9. Channel Discriminative Power (Fisher Ratio)

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$F_j = \frac{\mathrm{Between\text{-}Class\ Variance}}{\mathrm{Within\text{-}Class\ Variance}} = \frac{\sum_{c=1}^{K} n_c \left(\mu_{cj} - \mu_j\right)^2 \,/\, (K - 1)}{\sum_{c=1}^{K} \sum_{i \in c} \left(\phi_{ij} - \mu_{cj}\right)^2 \,/\, (N - K)}$$

- $K$: Number of gesture classes.
- $n_c$: Number of pure feature windows belonging to class $c$.
- $N$: Total number of clean windows across all classes ($N = \sum n_c$).
- $\mu_{cj}$: Mean of feature $\phi$ for channel $j$ in class $c$.
- $\mu_j$: Grand mean of feature $\phi$ for channel $j$ across all classes.
- $\phi_{ij}$: Feature value of channel $j$ for window $i$ in class $c$.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Different forearm muscle compartments activate preferentially during specific hand and wrist gestures (e.g., *Flexor Carpi Radialis* during wrist flexion vs. *Extensor Digitorum* during finger extension). The Fisher Ratio quantifies how effectively a specific electrode channel differentiates between target gestures relative to its internal trial-to-trial noise.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
- Channels with high Fisher Ratios ($F_j \gg 1.0$) are prime candidates for classification features.
- Channels with low Fisher Ratios ($F_j \approx 0$) contribute little discriminatory power and can be pruned during sensor reduction optimization.

### 10. High-Dimensional Gesture Separability (Mahalanobis Distance)

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\Sigma_w = \frac{1}{N - K} \sum_{c=1}^{K} \sum_{i \in c} (\boldsymbol{\phi}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_c)(\boldsymbol{\phi}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_c)^{\mathsf{T}} + 10^{-6} \mathbf{I}$$

$$d_{ab} = \sqrt{(\boldsymbol{\mu}_a - \boldsymbol{\mu}_b)^{\mathsf{T}} \, \Sigma_w^{-1} \, (\boldsymbol{\mu}_a - \boldsymbol{\mu}_b)}$$

- $\boldsymbol{\phi}_i \in \mathbb{R}^D$: Feature vector of log-RMS amplitudes across all $D$ channels for window $i$.
- $\boldsymbol{\mu}_a, \boldsymbol{\mu}_b \in \mathbb{R}^D$: Centroid vectors for gestures $a$ and $b$.
- $\Sigma_w \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times D}$: Pooled within-class covariance matrix.
- $+ 10^{-6} \mathbf{I}$: Tikhonov regularization term preventing singularity inversion errors.
- $d_{ab}$: Pairwise Mahalanobis statistical distance.

```
Multivariate Gesture Clustering & Separability Boundary:
       Channel 2 (Log-RMS)
          ▲
          │           (Gesture A)
          │            ╭───────╮
          │            │   ●   │ ◄── Centroid μ_a
          │            ╰───────╯
          │                  ▲
          │                   \  Mahalanobis Distance (d_ab)
          │                    \ (Scaled by Pooled Covariance Σ_w)
          │                     ▼
          │                   ╭───────╮
          │                   │   ●   │ ◄── Centroid μ_b
          │                   ╰───────╯
          │                  (Gesture B)
          └────────────────────────────────────────► Channel 1 (Log-RMS)
```

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Unlike standard Euclidean distance, the Mahalanobis distance accounts for channel variance scaling and inter-electrode covariance (cross-talk correlations). It measures the statistical separation between gesture clusters in multidimensional EMG space.

#### 📊 Separability Assessment Benchmark
| Mahalanobis Distance ($d_{ab}$) | Clinical & ML Assessment | Expected Classifier Impact |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **$d < 1.0$** | 🔴 Heavy Overlap | Frequent misclassifications; gestures are neuromuscularly indistinguishable. |
| **$1.0 \le d < 2.0$** | 🟡 Moderate Overlap | Moderate classification error; requires non-linear kernels or additional features. |
| **$2.0 \le d < 3.0$** | 🟢 Separable | Good classification boundary; linear models perform well. |
| **$d \ge 3.0$** | 🌟 Well Separated | Robust, highly distinct neuromuscular patterns; near-zero confusion. |

### 11. Repeatability & Coefficient of Variation (CV)

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{T-1}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(r_t - \bar{r})^2}, \qquad \mathrm{CV} \, (\%) = 100 \cdot \frac{s}{|\bar{r}|}$$

- $r_t$: Centered RMS of a channel for a specific gesture in trial $t$.
- $T$: Total number of recorded trials ($T \ge 2$).
- $\bar{r}$: Mean activation RMS across all trials for that gesture.
- $\mathrm{CV}$: Coefficient of variation expressing trial-to-trial variability as a percentage.

#### 🫀 Physiological & Physical Meaning in EMG
Evaluates the subject's motor consistency and stability of the physical skin-electrode interface across successive trial repetitions.

#### ⚠️ How this Affects the Analysis
- **$\mathrm{CV} < 20\%$**: Indicates excellent repeatability and reliable user execution.
- **$\mathrm{CV} > 50\%$**: Indicates user fatigue, inconsistent effort, or physical electrode migration during the session.

### 12. Automated Channel Quality Scorecard & Verdict Engine

#### 📐 Mathematical Formulas

$$\mathrm{Score}_c = \max\left(0, \; 100 - \sum_{k} p_k \cdot I_k\right)$$

- $\mathrm{Score}_c$: Final numerical quality score for channel $c \in [0, 100]$.
- $p_k$: Penalty points assigned to problem $k$.
- $I_k \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary indicator flag triggering if test condition $k$ is breached.

#### 📋 Deductive Scoring Rule Table

| Diagnostic Problem ($k$) | Trigger Condition | Points Lost ($p_k$) | Severity | Primary Corrective Action |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **`LOW_SIGNAL_VARIATION`** | $\sigma < 1.0\text{ counts}$ | **30** | High | Re-attach electrode; inspect channel wiring. |
| **`LOW_DYNAMIC_RANGE`** | $\mathrm{P2P} < 20\text{ counts}$ | **20** | Medium | Check pre-amplifier gain and skin contact. |
| **`ADC_SATURATION`** | $\mathrm{Sat} > 1.0\%$ ($>5.0\%$) | **30** (or **45**) | High | Reduce analog gain; check for baseline rail pegging. |
| **`ZERO_VALUES`** | $\mathrm{Zero} > 1.0\%$ ($>10.0\%$) | **25** (or **45**) | High | Re-seat electrode; inspect ground reference lead. |
| **`WEAK_REST_SEPARATION`** | $\mathrm{AR} < 1.5$ | **20** | Medium | Reposition electrode directly over muscle belly. |
| **`HIGH_MAINS_NOISE`** | $\mathrm{Mains} > 15.0\%$ | **10** | Medium | Improve skin prep; use shielded cables; enable notch filter. |
| **`FLATLINE_SEGMENTS`** | $\mathrm{Flatline} > 5.0\%$ | **20** | High | Check for intermittent hardware disconnection. |

#### 🏷️ Grading System
- **GOOD** ($\ge 80\text{ pts}$): Signal is clean, responsive, and ready for advanced analysis.
- **FAIR** ($50 - 79\text{ pts}$): Moderate degradation present; usable with caution.
- **POOR** ($< 50\text{ pts}$): Channel is severely degraded or defective; recommend exclusion.

## 🤖 Report Generation & AI Verification Safeguards

`Analysis.ipynb` features an automated reporting engine that compiles all analyses into a publication-grade PDF document via **ReportLab**.

### 1. Explanation Engine Options
1. **Rule-Based Engine (Default, Offline, Free)**:
   - Uses parameterized clinical templates directly populated with computed numerical outputs.
   - **100% deterministic and free of hallucinations**; operates with no API keys or internet connection.
2. **Google Gemini API (`gemini-2.5-flash` / Free Tier)**:
   - Generates contextual clinical narratives from structured numerical summaries.
3. **Anthropic Claude API (`claude-3-7-sonnet` / Paid Tier)**:
   - Generates advanced physiological biomechanics evaluations.

### 2. Hallucination Safeguard & Verification Protocol
When external LLM providers (Gemini/Claude) are enabled, an anti-hallucination verification pipeline protects data integrity:
- **Numerical Fact Extraction**: Every numerical scalar computed in Python is registered into a verified facts table.
- **Answer Validation**: The LLM's response is scanned for numerical figures. Any unverified numerical claim triggers an automatic retry.
- **Deterministic Redaction**: If a retry still contains unverifiable numbers, the offending sentence is programmatically stripped.
- **Code Autonomy**: Tables, charts, scores, and verdicts are generated purely by Python code and are never delegated to the language model.

## 💾 Input Data Specifications

The analyzer expects CSV files formatted according to standard biomedical acquisition schemas:

### 1. Primary Data File (`emg_data_*.csv`)
```csv
Timestamp,Packet_Number,EMG1,EMG2,EMG3,EMG4,EMG5,EMG6,EMG7,EMG8,Label,Trial
0,1001,512,508,515,510,511,514,509,513,Rest,1
2,1002,514,509,516,511,510,515,508,512,Rest,1
4,1003,720,685,810,750,530,525,515,520,Fist,1
```
- **`Timestamp`**: Monotonic time in milliseconds ($\mathrm{ms}$).
- **`Packet_Number`** *(Optional)*: Microcontroller transmission index.
- **`EMG1` - `EMG8`**: Raw integer ADC counts across channels.
- **`Label`**: Active gesture state (e.g., `Rest`, `Fist`, `WaveIn`, `WaveOut`, `Open`).
- **`Trial`**: Repetition sequence index ($1, 2, \dots, T$).

### 2. Metadata File (`metadata_*.txt`, Optional)
```text
Sampling_Rate_Hz: 500
ADC_Resolution_Bits: 10
Mains_Frequency_Hz: 50
Expected_Samples: 30000
Subject_ID: S01
Session: Pre-Fatigue
```
